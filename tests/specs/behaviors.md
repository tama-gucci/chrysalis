Tests prove deterministic boundaries and native transport. They do not prove semantic judgment, model conversation, real account authentication or desktop rendering. No UI/account success is inferred from a clean package or a successful schema test.

Pending onboarding conversation scenarios use synthetic context and the actual life profile:

- Give a goal and an available-time answer together; ask about a milestone only after the route
  prerequisite is chosen. Limit each round to one to three independent questions.
- Answer only one question in a round; retain the other as open and resume without repeating the
  settled answer or inventing the missing one.
- Reuse one availability constraint across two candidate projects; changing it reopens affected
  downstream decisions while preserving unrelated answers and applied records.
- Supply stale or unsupported document evidence; report waiting_for_evidence and continue an
  unrelated ready branch without fabricating lookup, browsing or sub-agent activity.
- Skip/defer the optional document folder; do not scan it, infer its contents or force downstream
  document outcomes. Keep roadmap, possible project and atomic-note choices distinct.
- Confirm a recap with an unresolved verification step; draft only the chosen scoped next step.
  Do not treat yes, an empty frontier or shared_understanding.status as mutation approval.
- Change a confirmed answer or reveal conflicting evidence before application; mark the affected
  recap/pending proposal stale and require new exact review. Each new atomic note still needs
  complete individual vetting followed by batch approval.

Source integration acceptance includes synthetic native setup/cancel/disconnect, protected credentials, account-scoped read-only Tasks envelopes, Health/Maps native-only evidence, strict schemas/HTTP bounds and actual codemode/stdio/nested gating. Live auth, real terminal rendering and model-guided source onboarding remain separate. Token comparisons estimate context rather than billed usage.
