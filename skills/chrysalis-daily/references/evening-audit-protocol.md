and native timeEntries; missing/invalid timing requires review, never fabricated learning.

unsuccessful-block rollover, three-step Starter Wedges for todo tasks aged at least 48h with estimate
at least 45m, and unresolved prior-cycle feedback pause. Review before application; no passive decay.

halt unrelated core work. Use the knowledge pipeline for new/revised items, including its review gate.

future items in their project ledger. Reconcile completed deliverables into project and Life-Roadmap
status as proposed changes; preserve completed work and local edits. Apply only reviewed changes.

before work: maintenance re-enters evening, rest re-enters morning, vacation waits until its date,
flow requires manual resumption, and auto_unresponsive requires user confirmation. Automatic cycle
resumption is a proposed state change under the previously approved pause policy, never an invented
active schedule. A successful audit result/proposal suppresses fallback even while application awaits
approval; execution entry alone does not. Report saved results and remaining decisions distinctly.
