COURSE OVERVIEW:
  CS 410 covers theoretical and practical foundations of fault-tolerant distributed
  systems. Topics include logical time, vector clocks, state machine replication,
  consensus (Paxos, Raft, Byzantine fault tolerance), distributed transactions
  (2PC, Spanner), and consistency models (linearizability vs. eventual consistency).

PREREQUISITES:
  CS 241 (System Programming) and CS 374 (Algorithms & Models of Computation).

GRADING POLICY:
  - Problem Sets (20%): Mathematical proofs and protocol traces.
  - Programming Labs (30%): Go-based implementations of distributed protocols.
  - Midterm Exam (20%): In-class theoretical examination.
  - Final Project (30%): Complete distributed key-value store with consensus.
